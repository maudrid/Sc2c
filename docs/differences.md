# Differences from UQM 0.8.0

Every deliberate difference between Attack on Crateris and vanilla UQM 0.8.0: what UQM does, what Crateris does, and why. Reference source: official UQM git, branch `v0.8.0`, commit `199e3748`; paths are relative to `sc2/`.

**Status:** *Planned* means decided in `docs/design.md` but not built yet. *Done* means built and covered by tests. Bug and crash fixes are listed here too.

## Co-op rules

| # | Topic | UQM 0.8.0 | Crateris | Why | Status |

| 1 | When the game clock runs | Ticks only in hyperspace and QuasiSpace (5 s per day, `HYPERSPACE_CLOCK_RATE`) and in interplanetary flight (30 s per day, `INTERPLANETARY_CLOCK_RATE`): `src/uqm/starcon.c`, `src/uqm/planets/solarsys.c`. Stopped in battles, conversations, orbit, on planets and at the starbase | One shared clock at a constant rate (seconds per game day, a host setting), in every activity, solo or co-op. Only story conversations and events and the host's pause stop it | Per-activity rates make a group burn game days faster than a solo player, because someone is nearly always flying (including frequent QuasiSpace trips). A constant rate is simple and keeps deadlines the same for any group size. Applies in solo play too | Done in stage 1 (hyperspace): constant rate, default 5 s per day as a placeholder setting. Story pauses stop it since stage 3a (row 2), the host's pause since stage 4a (item 10, row 12). It runs on through battles (stage 4a), which UQM's stops |
| 2 | Story conversations and events | Shown to the only player | Pause the game for everyone and show it to all; whoever started it chooses. Which talks are story is each conversation's rule (`Comm/TalkRules.cs`, stage 3, decision 6). While one goes on, the clock, every ship, every battle (its players watching the talk and fighting on after: stage 4, decision 11; a player in a battle can't start one), the landers on their surfaces and the orbit menus wait, and input meant for then is dropped (a star's entry already playing finishes on the screens, and an undocking scene plays, without the ship moving); a player who joins watches it, and everyone is shown the reply chosen.
