# Differences from UQM 0.8.0

Every deliberate difference between Attack on Crateris and vanilla UQM 0.8.0: what UQM does, what Crateris does, and why. Reference source: official UQM git, branch `v0.8.0`, commit `199e3748`; paths are relative to `sc2/`.

**Status:** *Planned* means decided in `docs/design.md` but not built yet. *Done* means built and covered by tests. Bug and crash fixes are listed here too.

## Co-op rules

| 1 | When the game clock runs | Ticks only in hyperspace and QuasiSpace (5 s per day, `HYPERSPACE_CLOCK_RATE`) and in interplanetary flight (30 s per day, `INTERPLANETARY_CLOCK_RATE`): `src/uqm/starcon.c`, `src/uqm/planets/solarsys.c`. Only story conversations and events and the host's pause stop it | Per-activity rates make a group burn game days faster than a solo player, because someone is nearly always flying (including frequent QuasiSpace trips). | 2 | Story conversations and events | Shown to the only player | Pause the game for everyone and show it to all; whoever started it chooses.
