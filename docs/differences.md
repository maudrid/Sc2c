# Differences from UQM 0.8.0

Every deliberate difference between Attack on Crateris and vanilla UQM 0.8.0: what UQM does, what Crateris does, and why. Reference source: official UQM git, branch `v0.8.0`, commit `199e3748`; paths are relative to `sc2/`.

**Status:** *Planned* means decided in `docs/design.md` but not built yet. *Done* means built and covered by tests. Bug and crash fixes are listed here too.

## Co-op rules

| # | Topic | UQM 0.8.0 | Crateris | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When the game clock runs | Ticks only in hyperspace and QuasiSpace (5 s per day, `HYPERSPACE_CLOCK_RATE`) and in interplanetary flight (30 s per day, `INTERPLANETARY_CLOCK_RATE`): `src/uqm/starcon.c`, `src/uqm/planets/solarsys.c`. Stopped in battles, conversations, orbit, on planets and at the starbase | One shared clock at a constant rate (seconds per game day, a host setting), in every activity, solo or co-op. Only story conversations and events and the host's pause stop it | Per-activity rates make a group burn game days faster than a solo player, because someone is nearly always flying (including frequent QuasiSpace trips). A constant rate is simple and keeps deadlines the same for any group size. Applies in solo play too | Done in stage 1 (hyperspace): constant rate, default 5 s per day as a placeholder setting. Story pauses and the host's pause come later |
| 2 | Story conversations and events | Shown to the only player | Pause the game for everyone and show it to all; whoever started it chooses | Shared story, shared consequences | Planned |
| 3 | Time skips (the starbase's building and the bomb's install, 14 days each; the Talking Pet) | `MoveGameClockDays` while the single player watches (`DoTimePassage` in `src/uqm/starbase.c`: the screen fades to black over two seconds) | Same skip, but as a story pause shown to every player | It moves time for the whole group | The starbase's two weeks done in stage 3a (item 5): the 14 days pass for everyone after its two-second fade; the story pause that shows it to everyone is item 6. The rest planned |
| 4 | Retreat by disconnecting | Retreat (`RunAwayAllowed` / `DoRunAway` in `src/uqm/battle.c`, fuel cost in `src/uqm/pickship.c`) is allowed only in encounters and the last battle, after the starbase is available, when not carrying the bomb. Fleeing with the flagship as the last ship costs 5 fuel units, down to no lower than 0 | Retreat itself is unchanged and works per player. A player who disconnects mid-battle retreats automatically with the same fuel cost, even where UQM doesn't allow retreat | A disconnected player's ship has to leave the battle | Planned |
| 5 | Battle sides | 2 sides, 2 players (`NUM_SIDES 2`, `NUM_PLAYERS 2` in `src/uqm/init.h`); one ship per side at a time | Several player ships at once against one enemy ship at a time. How other players join is row 15 | Co-op combat | Planned |
| 6 | Battle camera | One camera zooms to frame both ships | Each player has their own camera on their ship, plus an arena minimap | Players can be far apart in a bigger arena | Planned |
| 7 | Arena size | Fixed | Bigger | Room for up to 4 player ships | Planned. Size to set |
| 8 | Losing a ship | Flagship destroyed is game over; escort losses are permanent | A player's ship becomes a wreck; a rescuer revives it by flying to it and transferring crew, keeping at least 1 for themselves. Only flagship destruction ends the game | Players shouldn't be knocked out of a shared game | Planned |
| 9 | Escorts | Combat-only ships in `built_ship_q` (max 12): no fuel, cargo or landers, flown only in battle | Guest players captain escorts that fly, land, mine and talk like small flagships, with a smaller module list. The host's AI fleet stays as in vanilla | Each guest needs a full ship of their own | Planned. Module list to set |
| 10 | Key story items | Device flags on the single flagship (`*_DEVICE` game states) | Shared group inventory; plot checks look at the group | Story items shouldn't be stuck with a player who left | Planned |
| 11 | Money | One resource-unit balance (`SIS_STATE.ResUnits`) | Separate wallet per player, transfers allowed | Each player earns and spends | Planned |
| 12 | Pause | The player pauses any time | Only the host can pause; pause freezes everyone, including combat | Fairness in shared play | Planned |
| 13 | Saves | Single-player save format (`src/uqm/save.c`) | New host save format holding world state and every player's state, including absent players | Drop-in / drop-out | Planned |
| 14 | Starting a ship | The one player starts the game at the starbase in the flagship | A guest joining a host's game for the first time gets a new escort at the host's position. Only the joining player sees a short scene of the escort undocking from the host's flagship; nobody else is interrupted. If the host isn't flying (battle, talk, orbit, planet, starbase), the escort appears at the host's position in the surrounding hyperspace or solar system. A rejoining player returns where they left | Puts a new player straight into the group's game, without a trip from the starbase | Done in stage 1, with a placeholder undocking scene (a 2-second message) and placeholder escort loadout. Stage 2: in a system the escort undocks there; with the host in orbit, in the inner system next to the host's world |
| 15 | Joining a battle | A battle starts when an alien ship reaches the player (`encounter_collision` in `src/uqm/hyper.c`, `ip_group_collision` in `src/uqm/ipdisp.c`); nobody else can join | While a battle runs, a marker stays where it started, in the solar system or in hyperspace, visible to other players there. Flying into the marker joins the battle, the same way an alien ship starts an encounter. No fixed "nearby" radius | Lets the group help each other using a rule players already know | Planned |
| 16 | Leaving and disconnecting | One player; quitting ends the game | A player can leave at any time. Their ship, crew and cargo wait in the host's save; on disconnect the ship vanishes (mid-battle: row 4) and returns on rejoin. A dead player's wreck stays | Drop-in / drop-out | Done in stage 1 for hyperspace: a leaving player's ship stops and waits, and comes back stopped. Wrecks come with combat |
| 17 | Talking to aliens | One player talks to anyone | Ordinary conversations: one player per alien race at a time (dialogue lock), released on disconnect. Story conversations: row 2 | Stops two players changing one race's state at once | Planned |
| 18 | Battle salvage | `UninitEncounter` (`src/uqm/encount.c`): on victory only, the player gets `RACE_SHIP_COST / 8` resource units per enemy ship destroyed | Same amount, split equally between the players in the battle when it's won. Retreated and disconnected players get none | Fair split for a shared fight | Planned. Rounding of remainders to set |
| 19 | Melnorme credits | One balance, stored as game state (`MELNORME_CREDIT0`/`1` in `src/uqm/globdata.h`) | One balance per player, transferable between players at any time, including during a conversation | Each player sells their own bio data; others can top up a trader | Planned |
| 20 | Planet landing | One player, one lander | Players on the same planet share one landing screen, each with their own lander; each node is taken once | Lets players explore a planet together | Done in stage 2b |
| 21 | Chat | None | Text chat between players, in every activity | Co-op needs a way to talk | Planned |
| 22 | Cargo trading | One ship holds all minerals and bio data (`ElementAmounts`, `TotalBioMass` in `SIS_STATE`) | Players in the same solar system can trade cargo, limited by the receiver's free storage | Lets a group pool minerals for one player to sell | Planned |
| 28 | The rotating planet's face | Each time the player enters orbit the planet starts at the same frame (`InitSphereRotation` in `src/uqm/planets/pl_stuff.c`) and turns one map column every `PLANET_ROTATION_RATE` from there (`RotatePlanetSphere`) | The planet turns by the shared world clock at UQM's rate (`PlanetSphere.StepAt`): which face is at the front depends only on the time, not on when the player arrived | With each player's planet turning from their own arrival, two players orbiting the same world see different sides of it. Turning by the shared clock shows them the same world. Only the starting face differs from UQM; the speed, direction and drawing are UQM's | Done in the content stage (item 7) |
| 29 | The planetside terrain | The 4x map the lander drives over is made from the world's height map on entering its orbit (`TopoScale4x` in `src/uqm/planets/plangen.c`) with a little random variance from `frandom`, whose seed starts at `0x12345678` when UQM starts and is never reset, so a world's terrain depends on every landable world orbited before it since UQM started (orbiting a shielded world or a gas giant doesn't move `frandom`), and orbiting the same world again gives different terrain | `frandom` starts afresh for each world (`PlanetsideMap`), so a world's terrain is always the same, for every player on every visit. It's UQM's only when the world is the first landable one orbited since UQM started; a later world, or a second visit, differs from UQM's | Players landing together see the same terrain; nothing collides with it, so only the looks differ (stage 2b, decision 1) | Done in stage 2b |
| 30 | Creatures with several landers | Creatures react to the one lander, wherever it is (`object_animation` in `src/uqm/planets/lander.c`) | Each creature reacts to the nearest lander with crew (a wreck only if none has crew), measured the nearer way round east and west; the earlier lander wins a tie | Creatures that ignore a lander driving among them would be strange; enemy ships will target the nearest opponent too | Done in stage 2b |
| 31 | Hazards with several landers | Each frame, one roll for quakes, lightning and lava round the one lander (`BuildObjectList`, `AddGroundDisaster`, `AddLightning`) | One roll a lander a frame, at the world's chances, round that lander; its harmful lightning keeps to it and strikes only it; any lander touching a quake or lava is hurt by it. UQM's display queue holds 150 elements; each lander has that queue to itself (the nodes, creatures and its own bolts, explosion and hazards), so a group isn't short of hazards and each player's are UQM's | Each player's risk is UQM's, whoever else is landed | Done in stage 2b |
| 32 | Several landers on one surface | One lander | Every player orbiting a world lands on its one surface; a node is taken once, by the first lander to touch it (the earlier on the surface if two touch it in one frame); bolts don't hit other landers; one `TFB_Random` a surface, seeded from the frame and the world, where UQM's runs on from the program's start; a surface goes when its last lander leaves, so the next landing finds its creatures where UQM would put them again | Lets players explore a world together | Done in stage 2b |
| 33 | Leaving and saving while landed | The single player can't leave mid-landing; the game can't be saved on the surface | A player who leaves (or drops) while landed has their lander take off at once with its crew and cargo, like a retreat; a save keeps landed players in orbit with their landers' crew and cargo aboard (a lander whose crew are all dead is lost) | Nobody loses a lander by dropping out | Done in stage 2b |
| 34 | A new quake's first colour | A quake is drawn in its display primitive's last colour until its first new frame sets its own, and UQM never clears the primitives, so it's whatever an earlier screen or element left | Each surface starts with fresh primitives (black), then keeps UQM's reuse and colours: black on a fresh one, white after a quake, lightning or a flashing creature | Only the first three frames of a quake's look; nothing outside the surface is modelled | Done in stage 2b |
| 36 | Game-state fields that aren't story | One game state for the one player, including fields that hold what's happening now: where a talk is (`GLOBAL_FLAGS_AND_DATA`), how it ends (`BATTLE_SEGUE`), whether the allied commander has greeted the player this visit (`STARBASE_VISITED`), a battle's escape counter and planet (`ESCAPE_COUNTER`, `BATTLE_PLANET`), the lander's upgrades and shields, the Melnorme credits, which side of QuasiSpace the ship is on (`ARILOU_SPACE_SIDE`), whether a lander is down (`PLANETARY_LANDING`) and whether a visit changed its world (`PLANETARY_CHANGE`) (`src/uqm/globdata.h`) | Every other field is the group's one story state, the portal's countdown (`PORTAL_COUNTER`) among them, since the portal is the group's; these are kept per player, per ship or per talk (the lander's since stage 2b, the credits by row 19, the rest as stage 3a's talks and battles come) | Shared, one player's visit or battle would change another's | Done in stage 3a (item 1) for the story state; each field's own home comes with its activity Stage 3a item 5: `STARBASE_VISITED` before the alliance is the group's meeting the commander (the commander's talk reads it as "met before"; `GameWorld.CommanderMet`, saved), and each player's own only at the allied starbase |
| 37 | The starbase's gift | The allied commander's "the starbase is ready" gives the player 2,500 resource units (`starbas.c`) | Split equally between the players in the game when it's given; the starter keeps what doesn't divide (stage 3, decision 5). Each player's resource units are their own (row 11) | One starbase's supplies are the group's, as battle salvage is (row 18) | Done in stage 3a (item 5) |
| 38 | The Lunar base with several landers on Luna | One lander: picking the base up shows the lander report and sends the lander up (`GenerateSol_pickupEnergy`, `SetLanderTakeoff`) | The lander that picks it up does the same; the other landers there stay, and their players are shown the report with who found the base (stage 3, decision 9) | The discovery is one player's, but the group should know of it | Done in stage 3a (item 5) |
| 26 | ~~Remembering scans~~ | Nothing is remembered: each time a player orbits a world they scan it again (`ScanSystem` in `src/uqm/planets/scan.c`); only taken nodes are saved | **Withdrawn: same as UQM.** Scans aren't remembered. Each scan shows what's left at that moment, from the shared taken-node state, so it already reflects nodes a friend has just collected | Remembering scans added nothing: scanning is free, quick and always current, and nothing in the game would show a past scan | Withdrawn 2026-11-01 |

## Temporary differences

Differences that last only until a later stage provides what's missing. Numbered in the same series.

| # | Topic | UQM 0.8.0 | Crateris | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 23 | Touching a star in hyperspace | Decided by sprite masks: the star's centre point against the flagship's hyperspace sprite, and, for the star just left, its whole image against the sprite (`hyper_collision`, `hyper_death`, `AddStars` in `src/uqm/hyper.c`), as elements moving over the frame (`process.c`) | UQM's sprite intersection with the package's masks (`StarContact`, `Intersection`): the flagship's hyperspace frame, moving from where its sprite was to where it is, against each star near enough to be on its screen as a single point, and the star just left as its whole image. The other rules are UQM's: only while moving or steering, and not the star just left until the ship has cleared it | Stage 2 used circles because the masks are content. The intersection and the masks are checked against UQM's own code; the hyperspace collision loop around them isn't recorded from UQM (no trace runs UQM's display list), so when exactly in a frame a fast ship touches a star could differ by a frame | Done in stage 2 with circles; masks and UQM's intersection since content stage item 9 |
| 24 | Entering a star | The flagship spins and drifts onto the star for a few frames before the system loads (`init_transition`, `unhyper_transition`); the number of frames depends on the sprite's frame count | The server puts the ship in the system at once, but holds its system flight and ignores its input until the entry has played, as UQM does, and sends where and how the ship touched the star. Every client plays UQM's entry from that contact (`StarEntry`, `StarEntryTracker`): it turns to facing 0, plays the hyperspace sheet's entry frames and drifts onto the star at UQM's velocity, while its player's screen stays on hyperspace and its status says it's entering the star (18 to 33 frames after the contact, under 1.5 seconds); the host drops input read while the entry played (stamped for any frame up to the entry's end plus the input delay), however late it arrives, and doesn't repeat it on frames with no input, so keys held during it don't reach the system after it. Snapshots with entries are protocol version 3. A guest who joins while the host's entry plays rides in with the flagship (it isn't drawn entering itself), arrives with it, and sees its undocking scene in the system. The view doesn't scroll with the drifting ship as UQM's does | The server needn't wait for a client's animation; the ship arrives in the same place and the player sees the same entry | Done in stage 2; the client animation since content stage item 9 |
| 25 | ~~Touching a planet or moon in a system~~ | Decided by sprite masks: a world's centre point against the flagship's sprite for a new contact, and the world's whole image to tell when a contact has ended (`CheckIntersect`, `getCollisionFrame` in `src/uqm/planets/solarsys.c`). The masks come from the content package | **Closed: same as UQM.** UQM's own intersection with the package's masks (`IpFlight.CheckIntersect`, `Intersection`), checked frame by frame against UQM's flights recorded with the same masks | Stage 2 used circles until the content was read | Closed in content stage item 9 |
| 27 | Worlds that start a conversation | Touching a starbase, the Sa-Matra, the Umgah homeworld or the Slylandro gas giant starts a conversation or a battle instead of an orbit (`generateOrbital` in `src/uqm/planets/generate/`) | The ship stays in the inner system, touching the world, as if the conversation had ended at once. Earth's starbase starts its visit since stage 3a (item 5: the commander, the Ilwrath, the alliance, the starbase ready); while another player's story visit goes on, or at the allied starbase until its menu (item 8), touching it still does nothing | Conversations and battles don't exist yet | Done in stage 2; Earth's starbase replaced in stage 3a; the others by their conversations in stage 3b, and for the Sa-Matra (a battle) by combat in stage 4 |
| 39 | The Ilwrath ship at Earth | After the Ilwrath talk the player fights it: losing is game over, and there's no retreating before the alliance (`VisitStarBase`, `battle.c`) | The talk is played and its battle counts as won: the commander's next talk follows, as after a victory (stage 3, decision 3) | Combat is stage 4 | Done in stage 3a (item 5), temporary until stage 4 puts the real fight in (and decides who fights it) |
| 40 | The Ur-Quan drone at Earth | The drone orbits Earth until it has delivered its message (`gensol.c`'s `init_probe`, `urquanc.c`); then the commander can be asked about "the red thing" | There's no drone, so `PROBE_MESSAGE_DELIVERED` stays unset and that question isn't offered | Ships in space are stage 3b | Temporary until stage 3b |
| 41 | The allied commander's talk | After "the starbase is ready" the commander offers to buy the hold's minerals and to tell of the mission, the history and the races (`starbas.c`); the allied starbase has its menu (`DoStarBase`) | Only the goodbye is offered after "the starbase is ready", and touching the allied starbase does nothing | The rest of the talk and the menu are stage 3a's item 8 | Temporary until item 8 |
| 42 | Talks on the screen | The communication screen: the alien animated, the subtitles paged at the voices' pace, the replies (`comm.c`) | The talk's lines and replies are listed in the status panel, and the starter answers with up, down and Enter; only the starter sees the talk, other players' ships go on, and a starter who leaves undoes the visit. A save made during a visit is of the game before it, as UQM can't save in one | The screen is item 11; story pauses and passing the replies on are item 6 | Temporary until items 6 and 11 (the saving rule stays) |

| 35 | The lander's boarding, launch and return | Before the landing the crew board one by one in the gauges and the lander launches over the planet (`AnimateLanderWarmup`, `AnimateLaunch`); after the take-off it flies back into the ship | They take no time and aren't shown: the gauges show the crew aboard at once and the surface appears straight away; the landing's second of waiting above the site is UQM's | The landing starts on the shared surface at once; the animations are only client drawing, left for a later polish | Done in stage 2b, temporary |
